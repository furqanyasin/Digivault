2 The Problem

What was slowing us down & putting clients at risk.

Managing dozens of client sites across hosts, domains and logins meant constant blind spots — we often found out about problems after the client did.

Silent script changes

Third-party scripts (pixels, tag manager, chat) changed on client sites with zero visibility — a security, compliance and breakage risk.

Access scattered everywhere

"Do we even have access to this site?" Hosting, domain and login info lived in spreadsheets, chats and people's memory.

Missed renewals

Domains, SSL, hosting and contracts lapsing unnoticed — risking outages and lost domains.

Credentials shared unsafely

Client logins passed around in plaintext over chat and email — a serious security exposure.

No uptime / performance eyes

No proactive monitoring of downtime, speed or vulnerabilities — issues discovered late, reactively.

Manual client reporting

Pulling status together by hand for each client — slow, inconsistent, easy to skip.

4 Security

LastPass-grade vaulting. WP-management-grade site security. Built in.

The protection people trust from LastPass and ManageWP / MainWP — built into Digivault. For each area: what it means for you, and how it works under the hood.

Passwords kept in a locked vault

like LastPass

Client logins are stored in a sealed digital safe. Staff can use a password to log in without ever seeing or copying it, and you decide exactly who can access each one.

HowAES-256 authenticated encryption (Fernet); keys live only in environment variables, never in the database; key rotation & tamper detection; per-member sharing with optional hide-password; browser-extension autofill.

Client sites checked & guarded 24/7

like WP managers

Digivault constantly inspects each site for known weaknesses and missing protections, watches for tampering, and warns before certificates expire — like a guard doing regular rounds.

HowPlugin/theme vulnerability scanning; HTTP security-header grading (HSTS, CSP, X-Frame-Options…) with fixes; third-party script tamper/change detection; SSL & domain expiry monitoring.

Only the right people get in

2-step login

Everyone signs in with a password plus a one-time code from their phone, and each person sees only what their role allows. One client's data can never leak into another's.

HowShort-lived JWT sessions with refresh-token rotation; two-factor auth (TOTP) + backup codes; role-based & custom per-member permissions; strict multi-tenant data isolation.

Locked-down system, full paper trail

accountability

Everything runs over secure connections, abuse is blocked automatically, and every important action is recorded in a tamper-proof log — so nothing happens in the dark.

HowEnforced HTTPS/HSTS; secure cookies; API rate-limiting / throttling; immutable audit log of every significant action.
